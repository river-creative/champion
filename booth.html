Before anything else

Turn Admin on

Nothing can be entered or changed until you do.

  1. Open the Champion App.
  2. Tap ADMIN in the top right.
  3. Enter the PIN you were given at check-in.

With Admin on you'll see entry fields, the drag handle ⋮⋮, the feature star ☆, and delete buttons ✕. With it off the app looks exactly as attendees see it.

The PIN is not in this document. Don't write it on anything that stays at the booth, and don't leave a phone unlocked and unattended with Admin on. Anyone with the PIN can rewrite results for every station.
The PIN does two jobs. It unlocks the controls, and it authorises your device to publish. Enter it once per device per session — if your saves stop appearing on the Scoreboard, re-enter it first.

Scoring a match

The most common thing you'll do all weekend.

1
Open the tournamentBrackets tab, then the tournament by name.
2
Tap either competitor in the matchA confirmation opens. Tapping a name doesn't score anything on its own.
3
Enter both scores if the tournament tracks themThe dialog updates live to show who will advance.
4
ConfirmThe winner moves to the next round and the result publishes to every device within about three seconds.
The scores decide the winner, not the name you tapped. Tap Marcus Reed, then enter 7 for him and 21 for Jake Rollins, and Jake advances. The dialog tells you who's going through before you confirm — read that line. Ties and blank scores fall back to the name you tapped.

Featuring a match

Tap the ☆ on any unplayed match to mark it as the one to watch. It jumps to the front of Up Next everywhere, on phones and on the Scoreboard. One featured match per tournament — starring another moves it.

Fixing the wrong matchup

Drag one competitor's name onto another to swap them. You'll be asked to confirm, because it changes who plays whom. Use this when someone is entered in the wrong slot, before the match is played.

Brackets

Setting up a tournament

Entrants in, bracket out.

  1. Open the tournament and find the Entrants box (Admin only).
  2. Type or paste the names, one per line.
  3. Tap Apply.

The bracket regenerates from that list. If the count isn't a power of two the app adds byes automatically — a competitor with a BYE advances without playing, which is normal.

Apply wipes the tournament. Every score, every winner, every featured pick for that tournament is deleted and the tree is rebuilt from scratch. Use it to set up or to reset — never to add one late entry mid-competition, because you'll lose every result already recorded.

⋮⋮ Reorder

Press and drag the handle to move a competition up or down. The order you set here is the order the Scoreboard uses. Put whatever is running right now near the top.

☆ Feature

Promotes one competition to a full-width panel across the top of the Scoreboard, showing five places instead of the usual top five in a small card. Tap again to clear it. Only one at a time.

Adding a competition

Scroll to Add Competition, type the name exactly as it should appear on the big screen, then pick the type:

  • Timed — m:ss or seconds, fastest wins.
  • Weight — pounds, heaviest wins.
  • Count — reps or points, most wins.
  • Bench · DOTS — body weight and lift in pounds, ranked on DOTS points.

The type sets how the leaderboard sorts and which fields you get. It can't be changed afterwards — to switch, delete and recreate.

✕ deletes everything. Removing a competition takes its leaderboard and its whole update history with it. To empty a competition but keep it, use Clear results instead — see the next section.
Compete

Entering results

Three fields and a plus button.

  1. Open the competition.
  2. Type the competitor's name.
  3. Enter BW (body weight) if the competition asks for it, then the score.
  4. Tap +.

The leaderboard re-sorts immediately and the app writes an update automatically — a new leader, a move into the top five, a personal improvement. Those updates are what fill the Latest panel on the Scoreboard, so you rarely need to post anything by hand.

Corrections

Fixing a mistake

Typos happen at speed. All of them are recoverable.

Wrong score for someone already on the board

Re-enter the same name with the correct score and tap +. It overwrites their row rather than adding a second one. Names match regardless of capitalisation.

Someone shouldn't be on this leaderboard

Tap the ✕ at the end of their row and confirm. Only that result is removed.

A bad update is still showing on the big screen

Deleting a result does not delete the update it generated. If a bogus "New leader" line is still up there, scroll to Updates and tap the ✕ on that entry too. Fixing a bad score is usually two deletions — the row and the update.

Start this competition over

Clear results, under the entry fields, empties the leaderboard and the updates while keeping the competition itself — same name, same type, same settings. This is the one to use between sessions or when practice data needs to go.

Wrong winner advanced in a bracket

Reapply the entrants list to rebuild that tournament, then re-enter the results. There's no undo on a single match, so it's worth reading the confirmation line before you tap through.

The big screen

The Scoreboard

Runs itself once it's up. Your job is to get it up and leave it alone.

1
Open the Scoreboard on the display machineUse the address the tech team gives you — it's not linked from here or from the app.
2
Go fullscreenF11 on Windows, Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac. Hide the cursor by leaving it still.
3
Stop the display sleepingSet the machine to never sleep and disable the screensaver before the session starts, not during it.
4
Hard-refresh onceCtrl+Shift+R. This clears any old cached copy — the single most common reason a Scoreboard looks frozen.
5
Confirm it's liveEnter one test result at a booth and watch it appear. Then remove the test row.

What it does on its own

  • Featured competition — full width across the top, when one is starred. Leader centred, then 2 and 3, then 4 and 5.
  • Brackets — each tournament takes a turn every two minutes. Wide brackets pan across; ones that already fit light up round by round instead.
  • Leaderboards — top five of every competition, in the order you set with the drag handle.
  • Latest / Up Next — swaps every two minutes, holding still for 90 seconds then scrolling slowly for the last 30.
  • Up Next — one match from each bracket before any bracket repeats, later rounds first, featured matches first of all.

Semifinals and finals glow gold. New leaders, semifinal and final wins, and champions appear in gold in Latest. Nothing on the Scoreboard needs a click.

September 1

Clearing the practice data

Do this the day before, not on the morning of.

  1. Admin on.
  2. Each bracket → Entrants box → paste the real roster → Apply. This clears every practice result for that tournament.
  3. Each competition → Clear results → confirm.
  4. Delete competitions you don't need with ✕; add the ones you do.
  5. Set the running order with the drag handle, and star the competition that should lead the Scoreboard.
  6. Open the Scoreboard and confirm it matches.

Budget fifteen minutes. Doing it a day early means any surprise is a problem you have time to solve.

When something's off

Troubleshooting

My entries aren't reaching the Scoreboard

Re-enter the PIN — that's what authorises your device to publish. If it still doesn't stick, check you have a connection and try one entry again.

The Scoreboard is showing old data

Hard-refresh it: Ctrl+Shift+R. A cached page is far more common than a real fault.

Two people are entering the same competition

Last save wins. That's intentional — an entry never silently reverts under your hands — but agree who owns each station so you're not overwriting each other.

Everything looks broken

Close the tab, reopen the app, re-enter the PIN. Your data lives on the server, not on your phone — nothing is lost by reloading.

Still stuck

Get the tech team. Don't start deleting competitions to try to fix a display problem — that destroys real results and won't help.
