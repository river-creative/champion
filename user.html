Getting in

Open it and you're in

No login, no download, no code to enter.

  1. Open champion-conf.vercel.app in your phone's browser.
  2. That's it — you're looking at live results.
  3. Optional: tap your browser's share menu and choose Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

The app updates itself every few seconds. You never need to pull down to refresh or hunt for a reload button — if a match is scored at the pavilion, your screen catches up on its own.

Booth managers enter results. Bring anything that needs correcting to the crew at that station.

Bench press

How DOTS works

Why the biggest lift isn't automatically first place.

Raw pounds reward the heaviest guy in the room. DOTS is the formula competitive powerlifting uses to compare lifters fairly across body weights: it takes what you lifted, weighs it against what you weigh, and returns a single number of points.

  • Your row shows all three: body weight, weight lifted, and DOTS points.
  • Ranking is on points, never on raw pounds.
  • A 260 lb lifter pressing 340 can finish behind a 150 lb lifter pressing 260. That's the formula working, not a bug.
Three age categories. Bench press runs as 18–23, 24–45 and 45+, each with its own leaderboard and its own champion. Check you're looking at your category before you compare yourself to anyone.
In the room

The Scoreboard

The big screen. Same data as your phone, built to be read from the back row.

You don't need to do anything to use the Scoreboard — just look up. It runs itself and shows the same live results you have in your hand.

What it cycles through

  • Featured competition — one leaderboard promoted to full width across the top, when the crew has chosen one.
  • Brackets — each tournament takes its turn, panning across if it's too wide to fit, or lighting up round by round if it isn't.
  • Leaderboards — the top five of every competition.
  • Latest / Up Next — one panel that swaps every two minutes, scrolling gently before it changes.

Shorthand

What the colours mean

Two accents, used consistently everywhere.

Red

Live and active. Winners' names, scores, competition labels, the Up Next tag, and the pulsing outline on a semifinal or final match card.

Gold

First place and milestones. The leader of any leaderboard, a featured competition, a new #1, a semifinal or final win, and a champion crowned.

If something on screen is gold, it's worth looking at.

Quick answers

Common questions

My result isn't showing yet

Results appear once the crew at that station enters them, which can lag the actual lift or match by a few minutes. If it's still missing after that, ask at the booth.

The screen looks stuck

Close the tab and open the app again. It reloads in a second and picks up everything current.

Can I fix a wrong score?

Not from your phone — corrections go through the booth so the record stays consistent. Bring it to the crew at that station and they'll sort it.

Do I need wifi?

Any connection works, wifi or cellular. The app is small and light on data.

Where's the bracket I was watching?

Brackets tab, then pick the tournament by name. The app remembers nothing between visits, so it always opens on Home.
